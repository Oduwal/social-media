How it works

Every clip goes through you. Nothing is posted until you have watched it and pressed approve.

Step 1

Add a video

Point Clipper at a long video you own: a stream, podcast, interview or vlog.

Step 2

Review the highlights

Clipper suggests the strongest moments, cut to vertical 9:16 with captions. Watch each one, edit the caption, or throw it away.

Step 3

Approve and post

Choose who can see the post and whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed, then approve. Clipper uploads it to your connected TikTok account.

Features

Built for creators who make long videos and want consistent short-form output without hours of editing.

Highlight detection

AI looks through the full video and picks out the moments people actually react to.

Vertical reframing

Clips are reframed for phone screens so the speaker stays in shot.

Captions

Burned-in captions and suggested post captions, which you can rewrite before posting.

Clip library

Track which clips are ready, which are waiting for review and which have been posted.

Post to TikTok

Connect your account with Login Kit for TikTok and publish through the official Content Posting API.

You stay in control

Privacy level, comment, duet and stitch settings are chosen by you on every post.

TikTok integration

Clipper uses TikTok's official developer products only.

Login Kit

You sign in with TikTok and grant permission. Clipper never sees your TikTok password.

Content Posting API

Approved clips are uploaded to the account you connected and to no other account.

Disconnect any time

Disconnecting in Clipper deletes the stored tokens. You can also revoke access in TikTok's settings.

FAQ

Does Clipper post without asking me?

No. Each clip is posted only after you review and approve it.

Which videos can I use?

Only videos you own or have the rights to post. You are responsible for following TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Music Usage Confirmation.

What TikTok data does Clipper store?

Your TikTok open ID, display name and the access tokens needed to post. See the Privacy Policy for details.
