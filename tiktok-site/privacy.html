Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What Clipper is

Clipper is a desktop application that prepares video clips and posts them to the user's own TikTok account.

What we collect

When you connect TikTok, the app receives your TikTok open ID, display name and the access tokens needed to post. Nothing else about your account or any other user is collected.

How it is used

These details are used only to upload videos you have approved to the connected TikTok account.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored only on your own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by us, and they are not sold, shared or disclosed to third parties. The app does not collect data from other TikTok users.

Removing access

You can disconnect TikTok in the app at any time, which deletes the stored tokens, or revoke access in TikTok under Settings, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions.
