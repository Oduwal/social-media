Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What Clipper is

Clipper is a personal desktop application used by its owner to prepare video clips and post them to the owner's own TikTok account.

What we collect

When the owner connects TikTok, the app receives their TikTok open ID, display name and the access tokens needed to post. Nothing else about the account or any other user is collected.

How it is used

These details are used only to upload videos the owner has approved to the connected TikTok account.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by us, and they are not sold, shared or disclosed to third parties. The app does not collect data from other TikTok users.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect TikTok in the app at any time, which deletes the stored tokens, or revoke access in TikTok under Settings, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions.
